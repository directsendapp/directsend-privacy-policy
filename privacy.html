Privacy Policy

DirectSend - Chat for WA

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

DirectSend - Chat for WA ("we", "our", or "the app") is a messaging utility designed to help users start WhatsApp conversations without saving phone numbers as contacts.

1. Information We Collect

DirectSend does not operate a remote customer database and does not require users to create an account.

We do not directly collect or upload your name, email address, password, contacts, messages, photos, or files to a DirectSend remote server.

2. Phone Numbers

When you enter a phone number, it is used to create a WhatsApp chat link or to perform the action selected by the user.

With the user's consent, up to 5 recently used phone numbers may be stored locally on the user's device for convenience.

This local history is not uploaded to or stored on a DirectSend remote server.

3. Clear History

Users can delete their locally stored recent phone number history at any time by using the "Clear History" option available in the app.

4. WhatsApp and Other Services

When you choose to open a WhatsApp chat, the phone number and message may be processed by WhatsApp or another device/browser service used to complete the requested action.

WhatsApp is a separate service operated by Meta Platforms, Inc. Your use of WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

5. Location Permission

The Location feature may request location permission from your device. Location information is used only for the functionality selected by the user.

6. Images and Documents

Selected images and documents are used only for selection and preview within the web page.

DirectSend does not upload selected images or documents to a remote DirectSend server.

7. Data Storage

DirectSend does not maintain a personal remote database of user phone numbers, messages, images, or documents.

Any recent phone number history stored by the app is stored locally on the user's device and can be cleared using the Clear History option.

8. Permissions

Depending on the features used, the app may use normal device, browser, location, WhatsApp, phone, or other system functionality required to perform the action requested by the user.

9. Third-Party Services

DirectSend may open or interact with third-party services such as WhatsApp, browser services, phone or SMS services.

We do not control how third-party services collect, use, or process information. Their own privacy policies and terms apply to your use of those services.

10. Children's Privacy

DirectSend does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep DirectSend simple and privacy-conscious. However, no internet-based service or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DirectSend - Chat for WA, you can contact us through the support information provided with the application.
