Privacy Policy

DirectSend - Chat for WA

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

DirectSend - Chat for WA ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles information when you use DirectSend - Chat for WA.

1. Information We Collect

DirectSend - Chat for WA is designed to help users open WhatsApp chats without saving a phone number as a contact.

The app does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, password, or other personal information.

2. Phone Numbers

When you enter a phone number in the app, the number is used only to create a WhatsApp chat link and open the WhatsApp application.

Phone numbers entered by the user are not stored on our servers and are not collected by us.

3. WhatsApp

DirectSend - Chat for WA uses WhatsApp links to open conversations. WhatsApp is a separate service operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.

Your use of WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

4. Data Storage

DirectSend - Chat for WA does not maintain a personal database of the phone numbers entered by users.

5. Permissions

The app may use the device's normal web/browser functionality to open WhatsApp links. The app does not require access to your contacts, messages, photos, or files for its main functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

DirectSend - Chat for WA does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may open third-party applications such as WhatsApp. We do not control how third-party services collect or process information.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app simple and privacy-friendly. However, no internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DirectSend - Chat for WA, you can contact us through the app's available support channels.
