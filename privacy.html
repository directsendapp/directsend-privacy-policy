DirectSend Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

DirectSend is a messaging utility designed for quick communication. It does not operate a remote customer database.

1. Local Storage & Recent Numbers

If you provide consent, up to 5 recently used phone numbers may be stored in local storage on this device for your convenience.

You can delete this history at any time using the in-app Clear History option.

2. Third-Party Services

When you use WhatsApp or another device/browser service to initiate a chat or call, the phone number and message may be processed by that specific third-party service according to their respective policies.

3. Permissions & Features

  • Location: The Location feature may request location permission from your device only when you choose to share your location.
  • Files & Media: Selected images and documents are only selected/previewed locally in this web page. DirectSend does not upload them to a remote DirectSend server.
